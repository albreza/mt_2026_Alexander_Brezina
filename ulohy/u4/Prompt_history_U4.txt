Skupina 1 – Detail receptu
Názov: Pasta Carbonara
Fotky: 3 farebné plochy s ikonou jedla
Ingredients (názov – množstvo):
Spaghetti – 400 g
Egg yolks – 4 pcs
Guanciale – 150 g
Pecorino – 80 g
Black pepper – to taste
Salt – to taste
Nutrition facts – podnadpis per serving:
Energy – 620 kcal
Protein – 27 g
Fat – 24 g
Carbohydrates – 71 g
Method
Preparation
Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil.
Cut the guanciale into small strips.
Whisk the egg yolks with grated pecorino and black pepper.
Cooking 4. Cook the spaghetti until al dente. 5. Fry the guanciale in a dry pan until crispy. 6. Take the pan off the heat and add the drained pasta. 7. Pour in the egg mixture and stir quickly so the eggs do not scramble. 8. Add pasta water little by little until the sauce is creamy.
